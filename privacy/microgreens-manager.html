Privacy Policy — Microgreens Manager

Effective date: November 7, 2026 Publisher: White Wolf Tree Service LLC ("we," "us") Contact: whitewolfbackoffice@gmail.com

The short version

Microgreens Manager does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any of your personal information or the growing records you enter into the app. Everything stays on your device. The only time anything opens outside the app is when you tap a supplier link in the built-in supplies guide (which opens that supplier's public website in your browser) or tap "Email us for help" (which opens your own mail app).

What the app does with your data

All information you enter into Microgreens Manager (such as your farm name, trays and batches, harvests, loose and bagged/fridge stock, sales and customers, money entries, seed stock, supply-list ticks, and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage area on your own device. The app:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs;
  • requests no device permissions in the Android build and has no ability to access

your location, contacts, camera, or files outside the app's own storage;

  • makes no network connections of its own — it works fully offline, and nothing

you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

Voice entry (Tell and field dictation) is offered in the web version where your browser provides speech recognition; the app itself does not record, store, or transmit audio — it only files the words you confirm on the confirmation card. In the Android app the build declares no microphone permission, so voice shows an unavailable note and typing works exactly the same.

Because nothing is transmitted to us, we have no copies of your data, cannot see it, and cannot recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Microgreens Manager → Clear storage), your data is permanently deleted from your device. The app also provides an in-app control ("Erase everything") to erase all your data.

Supplier links in the supplies guide

The built-in supplies guide lists real seed, tray, and growing-supply suppliers with links to their public websites (checked October 30, 2026). Those links open only when you tap them, in your browser, and the app sends none of your records or information with them. What those websites do is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Documents and backups you make yourself

If you print a money summary, save one as a PDF, or use the app's backup export, those files are created on your device under your control. Where you store or send them is up to you, and this policy does not cover copies you choose to share.

Children's privacy

Microgreens Manager is a growing and selling record tool for small microgreens growers. It is not directed at children under 13, and — as explained above — it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Microgreens Manager changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the app's Play Store data safety declaration before that version is released.
