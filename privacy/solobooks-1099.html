Privacy Policy — SoloBooks 1099

Effective date: November 5, 2026 Publisher: White Wolf Tree Service LLC ("we," "us") Contact: whitewolfbackoffice@gmail.com

The short version

SoloBooks 1099 does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any of your personal information or the financial data you enter into the app. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does with your data

All information you enter into SoloBooks 1099 (such as income by payer/client, expenses, mileage trips, tax-year settings, tax-rate settings, estimated payments, and business/owner information) is stored only in the app's private storage area on your own device. The app:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs;
  • requests no device permissions and has no ability to access your location,

contacts, camera, microphone, or files outside the app's own storage;

  • makes no network connections and never connects to your bank — it works fully

offline, and nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Because nothing is transmitted to us, we have no copies of your data, cannot see it, and cannot recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → SoloBooks 1099 → Clear storage), your data is permanently deleted from your device. The app also provides an in-app control ("Erase everything") to erase all your data.

Documents and backups you make yourself

If you print a Schedule C summary or a tax worksheet, save one as a PDF, or use the app's backup export, those files are created on your device under your control. Where you store or send them is up to you, and this policy does not cover copies you choose to share.

Children's privacy

SoloBooks 1099 is a bookkeeping and tax-planning record tool for freelancers and one-person businesses. It is not directed at children under 13, and — as explained above — it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of SoloBooks 1099 changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the app's Play Store data safety declaration before that version is released.
