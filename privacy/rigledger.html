Privacy Policy — RigLedger

Effective date: November 10, 2026 Publisher: White Wolf Tree Service LLC ("we," "us") Contact: whitewolfbackoffice@gmail.com

The short version

RigLedger does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any of your personal information or the load, fuel, mileage, expense, and tax records you enter into the app. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does with your data

All information you enter into RigLedger (such as loads and brokers, routes, pay, fuel purchases by state, miles by state, expenses, the IFTA rates you type in, and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage area on your own device. The app:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs;
  • requests no device permissions and has no ability to access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files outside the app's own storage;
  • makes no network connections — it works fully offline, and nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Because nothing is transmitted to us, we have no copies of your data, cannot see it, and cannot recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → RigLedger → Clear storage), your data is permanently deleted from your device. The app also provides in-app controls to clear demo data and erase all data.

Documents and backups you make yourself

If you print an IFTA worksheet or tax summary, save one as a PDF, or use the app's backup export, those files are created on your device under your control. Where you store or send them is up to you, and this policy does not cover copies you choose to share.

Children's privacy

RigLedger is a bookkeeping tool for owner-operator truck drivers. It is not directed at children under 13, and — as explained above — it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of RigLedger changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the app's Play Store data safety declaration before that version is released.
