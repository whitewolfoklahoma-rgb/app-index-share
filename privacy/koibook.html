Privacy Policy — KoiBook

Effective date: November 2, 2026 Publisher: White Wolf Tree Service LLC ("we," "us") Contact: whitewolfbackoffice@gmail.com

The short version

KoiBook does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any of your personal information or the data you enter into the app. Everything stays on your device. The only time anything opens outside the app is when you tap a supplier link in the built-in supplies guide (which opens that supplier's public website in your browser) or tap "Email us for help" (which opens your own mail app).

What the app does with your data

All information you enter into KoiBook (such as your fish, pond, breeding, water-test, health, feeding, customer, waitlist and money records, settings, and photos you attach to fish records) is stored only in the app's private storage area on your own device. Photos you pick are read and shrunk on your device and stored with the fish record — they are never uploaded anywhere. The app:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs;
  • requests no device permissions and has no ability to access your location,

contacts, or files outside the app's own storage;

  • makes no network connections of its own — it works fully offline, and nothing

you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

Because nothing is transmitted to us, we have no copies of your data, cannot see it, and cannot recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → KoiBook → Clear storage), your data is permanently deleted from your device. The app also provides an in-app control ("Erase everything") to erase all your data.

Supplier links in the supplies guide

The built-in supplies guide lists real koi and pond suppliers with links to their public websites. Those links open only when you tap them, in your browser, and the app sends none of your records or information with them. What those websites do is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Documents and backups you make yourself

If you print a fish passport or sale certificate, export a backup, a water-test CSV, or a calendar (.ics) file, those files are created on your device under your control. Where you store or send them is up to you, and this policy does not cover copies you choose to share.

Children's privacy

KoiBook is a fish-keeping record tool for keepers, breeders and sellers. It is not directed at children under 13, and — as explained above — it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of KoiBook changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the app's Play Store data safety declaration before that version is released.
